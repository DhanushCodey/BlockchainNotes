## What is Layer 1?

L1 or Layer 1 is a blockchain which is in its purest form there are node that contributes to sebil resistance and help chain to reach consensus.

In other words, L1 is the base layer of the blockchain ecosystem that works without any plugins and also in which transactions are executed and confirmed.

Also known as settelment layer.

## What is layer 2 ?

L2 or Layer 2 is anything like applications or network that builds on top of the L1.
